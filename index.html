03Familia Code

Six Principles, One Squadron

Charter · 2020
P-01 · Excellence

Always push to be better.

We share replays, dissect mistakes, and improve together. Skill is a verb.

P-02 · Integrity

Letter and spirit.

We uphold the rules of the game and the rules of the squadron — even when no one is watching.

P-03 · Charity

Give back.

Mentor a newer pilot. Share your lineup. Spot a teammate before yourself.

P-04 · Loyalty

Show up.

Commit to the people who commit to you. The wing on your tag means something.

P-05 · Respect

Dignity, always.

Inside the squadron and outside it. Toxicity is not a strategy.

P-06 · Selfless Service

The team above the stat.

A capture is worth ten kills. The win is worth ten captures. The familia is worth all of it.
