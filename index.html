About Us

Who We Are

Premium Players Worldwide (PPW) is a War Thunder squadron and a familia — we've been playing together since 2020, and before that on World of Tanks. We're not just a tag; we're a tight-knit community.

Our main focus is Squadron Battles (also called Squadron Realistic Events — SQBs/SREs) and hanging out together — but we also run our own tournaments and take part in War Thunder's official TSS (Thunder Show Series) events. We're actively growing and looking for experienced players to compete and improve with.

If you bring skill, respect, and a willingness to learn, there's a place for you in our ranks.

Gameplay

What We Play

Our members span multiple nations and battle ratings. We primarily focus on Realistic and Simulator Battles but welcome anyone into our ranks.

🚀

Realistic Battles

Our bread and butter. Coordinate squad spawns, cap zones, and call out enemy positions in RB for the win.

🎮

Simulator Battles

For the hardcore pilots and tankers who want maximum immersion — we run SB squads regularly.

⚔️

Squadron Battles (SQBs/SREs)

Earn squadron research points in organised 8v8 competitive matches. Some call them SQBs, others SREs — either way, we run them regularly.

🌍

Ground Forces

Armoured warfare is at our core. We discuss tank lineups, BR meta, and flanking routes every day.

✈️

Air Forces

Propeller and jet aircraft pilots welcome. We share energy fighting tips, boom-and-zoom tactics, and CAS loadouts.

⚓

Naval Forces

Coastal and bluewater sailors who want to dominate the seas — we have a growing naval contingent.
