About Us

Who We Are

WTPPW is a War Thunder squadron that values both casual play and competitive performance. We welcome players of all skill levels who share a passion for combined arms combat and improving together.

We coordinate regular squadron battles, share research tips, and maintain a friendly environment where veterans help newcomers master each battle rating.

Our roster spans ground, air, and naval forces — giving every type of player a squad to roll with.

Gameplay

What We Play

Our members span multiple nations and battle ratings. We primarily focus on Realistic and Simulator Battles but welcome anyone into our ranks.

🚀

Realistic Battles

Our bread and butter. Coordinate squad spawns, cap zones, and call out enemy positions in RB for the win.

🎮

Simulator Battles

For the hardcore pilots and tankers who want maximum immersion — we run SB squads regularly.

⚔️

Squadron Battles

Earn squadron research points together in organised 10v10 competitive squadron battle sessions.

🌍

Ground Forces

Armoured warfare is at our core. We discuss tank lineups, BR meta, and flanking routes every day.

✈️

Air Forces

Propeller and jet aircraft pilots welcome. We share energy fighting tips, boom-and-zoom tactics, and CAS loadouts.

⚓

Naval Forces

Coastal and bluewater sailors who want to dominate the seas — we have a growing naval contingent.
