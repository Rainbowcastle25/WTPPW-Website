Rank System

Ranks & Progression

Ranks are awarded based on activity, contribution, and time with the squadron.

Commander

Squadron founder and leader. Sets direction, recruits officers, and manages the squadron in-game.

Officer

Trusted veterans who help run events, review applications, and represent the squadron in ranked play.

Sergeant

Experienced members who have proven their commitment and are working toward officer status.

Private

New recruits still in the introductory period. Play, contribute, and earn your promotion.
