06·CFAQ

Common Questions

Do I need to be in a specific time zone?

No — we have members across NA, EU, and a few in APAC. As long as you're around enough to actually fly with people, you're fine.

Can I be in another squadron at the same time?

No. In War Thunder you can only carry one squadron tag at a time, and we expect WTPPW members to be exclusive. If you're testing the waters, finish with your old squadron first.

Do I have to play SQBs / SREs?

No — SQBs are our focus but they're optional. Just be active in regular RB / SB and on Discord. The more you show up, the faster you promote.

What if my K/D is just barely under 1.0?

Apply anyway, mention it directly in your application. If the rest of the profile is strong, the recruitment officer will probably give you a trial.

How long until I get promoted?

Sergeant typically takes ~3-6 months of consistent activity. Officer is by invite only and based on trust + contribution to the squadron, not just stats.
