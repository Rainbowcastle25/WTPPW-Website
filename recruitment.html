Prerequisites

Requirements

We welcome players of all nations and play styles, but there are a few baseline requirements to keep the squadron active and enjoyable for everyone.

🎮

Active Player

Log in and play at least a few times per week. We're not hardcore, but idle accounts will eventually be removed.

🔢

Battle Rating

No strict BR requirement — we accept everyone from early-tier newcomers to top-tier veterans.

💬

Communication

You must be able to communicate in English (text is fine) and be willing to coordinate in squad battles.

🤝

Good Conduct

No toxic behaviour, team-killing, or harassment. Read our rules before applying.

🖥️

Discord

Discord is our primary communication platform. You must have a Discord account and join our server.

🌐

Any Nation

We have no nation restrictions. Play whatever tree you enjoy most — everyone is welcome.

Process

How to Apply

Follow these steps to join PPW. The whole process typically takes 1–3 days depending on officer availability.

  1. Read the Rules

    Before anything else, read our squadron rules carefully. All members are expected to follow them from day one.

  2. Join Our Discord

    Join the PPW Discord server and introduce yourself in the #introductions channel. Tell us your in-game name, your main nation, and what BR you play.

    Join Discord Server

  3. Post in #applications

    Fill in the application template in the #applications channel. Include your in-game username, K/D ratio, favourite vehicles, and why you want to join.

  4. Wait for Officer Review

    An officer will review your application and may ask follow-up questions. We aim to respond within 48 hours.

  5. Receive In-Game Invite

    If accepted, an officer will send you a squadron invite in-game. Accept it and you're officially a member of PPW!

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a certain K/D ratio to join?

No. We care more about attitude and willingness to improve than raw stats.

Can I be in another squadron?

War Thunder only allows one active squadron per account, so you would need to leave any existing squadron before joining.

Is a mic required?

No, text chat is perfectly fine. A mic is welcome but never mandatory.

What happens if I go inactive?

Members inactive for 30+ days without notice may be removed to keep the roster active. Just let an officer know if you need a break.

Can I reapply if rejected?

Yes, after a 14-day waiting period. Address any feedback given during the initial review before reapplying.

How long is the probation period?

New members hold the Private rank for approximately 2 weeks while officers assess activity and conduct.
