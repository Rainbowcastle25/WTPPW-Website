07·ACode of Conduct

Expected & Prohibited Behaviour

All Members
Authorised

Expected Behaviour

06 · Items
  1. Treat all members and opponents with respect at all times.
  2. Communicate clearly and constructively in battles and on Discord.
  3. Represent PPW with good sportsmanship in public lobbies.
  4. Follow officer instructions during organised squadron events.
  5. Report issues or concerns to an officer rather than handling them alone.
  6. Stay reasonably active — notify an officer before extended absences.
Restricted

Prohibited Behaviour

07 · Items
  1. Harassment, bullying, or targeted toxicity toward any player.
  2. Intentional team-killing or griefing of squadron members.
  3. Use of cheats, exploits, or third-party software that violates Gaijin's ToS.
  4. Sharing squadron internal information or plans with rival squadrons.
  5. Discriminatory language, hate speech, or political arguments.
  6. Spamming, advertising, or posting off-topic content in Discord channels.
  7. Posting NSFW, graphic, or sexually explicit content anywhere in the server.
07·BDisciplinary Procedure

Escalation Path

Applied Consistently
STAGE 01

Quiet Word

An officer takes you aside in DM. Most issues end here.

STAGE 02

Formal Warning

Recorded warning, logged. Two warnings inside 90 days escalate to the next stage.

STAGE 03

Suspension

Temporary removal from squadron ops and channels, typically 1–2 weeks. Squadron tag stays.

STAGE 04

Removal

Tag removed. For severe violations (cheats, harassment) we skip directly to this stage.

Disputes go to the Commander and Executive Officer. If you feel you've been treated unfairly, raise it — we'd rather have an awkward conversation than lose a good member to a misunderstanding.
