Who We Are

Our Values

PPW is built on six principles that guide every member — in battle and beyond. These aren't just words; they're the standard we hold ourselves to every day.

Six Principles

The PPW Standard

Every member of PPW is expected to uphold these values — whether you're leading a Squadron Battle, helping a recruit, or just chatting in Discord.

🏆

Excellence

Always push to be better.

  • Review your own replays and study what went wrong — growth starts with honest self-assessment.
  • Ask for loadout feedback and share advice freely; the whole squadron improves when individuals improve.
  • Always strive to improve your skills and game knowledge, whether that's learning a new BR range, a new nation, or better comms.
⚖️

Integrity

Uphold both the letter and the spirit of our rules.

  • No cheating, exploit abuse, or use of any software that violates Gaijin's Terms of Service — ever.
  • Be honest about your stats and skill level when applying; we'd rather know the truth than discover it later.
  • Never hide information that would affect a decision. Technically staying within the rules while deliberately misleading the squadron is still a breach of integrity.
🤲

Charity

Give back to the people around you.

  • Take time to teach newer members game mechanics — not everyone starts at the same level, and that's okay.
  • Share research tips, BR meta knowledge, and loadout advice without gatekeeping.
  • Keep your patience when teammates make mistakes. Everyone learns at their own pace — frustration helps no one.
🔗

Loyalty

Show up and stay committed.

  • If you sign up for a Squadron Battle, be there — no-showing without warning leaves your team a man short.
  • Stay reasonably active; if life gets in the way, let an officer know so we don't wonder where you went.
  • Don't abandon battles mid-match — see it through with your squad, regardless of the score.
🎖️

Respect

Treat everyone with dignity.

  • No trash talk to teammates or enemies — sportsmanship applies in every lobby, not just PPW events.
  • Offer constructive criticism only; there's always a way to give feedback that doesn't tear someone down.
  • Zero tolerance for harassment, discrimination, or hate speech — this is a welcoming community, full stop.
🤝

Selfless Service

Put the team above your own stats.

  • Cap zones, hold flanks, and play the objective — farming kills while your team loses a cap is not a PPW way to play.
  • Spot for teammates, call out threats, and cover retreats even when there's nothing in it for your score.
  • Step up when the squadron needs you — volunteer for officer duties, help organise events, or just be the first one to join a struggling squad.
