08·APrinciples

What We Stand For

6 · Charter Values
P-01
EX
// EXCELLENCE

Always push to be better.

We share replays, dissect mistakes, and study the meta together. Skill is a verb — practiced every match, every patch, every season.

P-02
IN
// INTEGRITY

Letter and spirit.

We uphold the rules of the game and the rules of the squadron — even when no officer is watching. Especially then.

P-03
CH
// CHARITY

Give back.

Mentor a newer pilot. Share your lineup. Spot a teammate before you spot yourself. We rise together or we don't rise at all.

P-04
LO
// LOYALTY

Show up.

Commit to the people who commit to you. The wing on your tag means you're not flying alone — and neither are they.

P-05
RE
// RESPECT

Dignity, always.

Inside the squadron and outside it. With teammates, opponents, and strangers in the channel. Toxicity is not a strategy.

P-06
SS
// SELFLESS SERVICE

The team above the stat.

A capture is worth ten kills. The win is worth ten captures. The familia is worth all of it.

"We are not just a tag on the side of a hangar. We are a familia — players who showed up for each other before the squadron existed, and who'll be flying together long after we hang up the joysticks. The wing of WTPPW is a promise to the person next to you."

— PPW Charter · 12 March 2020
